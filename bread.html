Mix 1/4 Cup of Mother Yeast with 1/4 Cup Water and 1/4 Cup Bread Flour. Let double in size (probably like 3 hours)

Mix Yeast with 4 Cups Flour, 1 3/4 Cup Water, 1 Tbsp Salt and cover with damp towel for an hour

Stretch and fold the dough then wait 20 minutes. Repeat 4 times.

Keep the dough on the counter for 12-30 hours

Now preheat the oven to 450F. Shape the dough and put in the cast iron on parchment paper. Put the dough in the oven with the lid on

Remove the lid after 40 minutes. Bake the dough for an additional 15 minutes.

Take it out of the oven and let it cool. Share it with your friends and don't judge yourself if it doesn't look or taste amazing
